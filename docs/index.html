Only Me

A private, local-only personal tracker. Tasks, finance, gym, snapshots, notes, vault — everything stays on your device. No accounts. No cloud. No tracking.

✅

Tasks & Streaks

Track daily habits and tasks with streak counters. Set reminders and never miss a beat.

💸

Finance

Log debts you owe and money owed to you. Track partial payments and clear balances.

🗓️

Events

Plan upcoming events with per-item budgets. Compare estimated vs. actual spend.

🏋️

Gym Plan

Build a weekly or 2-week workout split. Track sets, reps, and weight. Log bodyweight over time.

📸

Snapshots

A visual journal for progress photos. Pinch to zoom, swipe to compare, share with ease.

🔐

Vault

Store passwords and credentials locally. No sync, no breach risk — only on your device.

📝

Notes & Links

Quick-capture notes and bookmark links that open directly in your browser.

💾

Backup & Restore

Export your entire data as a single JSON file. Import on any device to restore instantly.

100% Private by design

Only Me has no internet permission, no analytics, no ads, and no backend. All your data lives in your phone's sandboxed storage and never leaves your device.
