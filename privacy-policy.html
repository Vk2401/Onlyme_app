🛡 Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Effective date: April 26, 2026 · Last updated: April 26, 2026

Short version: Only Me stores all your data locally on your device. We do not collect, transmit, or share any personal information with anyone — ever.

1. Introduction

Only Me is a personal tracker application developed by Webronic. This Privacy Policy explains what data the app stores, how it is stored, and your rights as a user.

2. Information the app stores

All of the following is stored locally on your device only — nothing is sent anywhere:

DataWhere storedPurpose
Tasks, debts, events, gym plan, weight logSharedPreferencesCore functionality
Snapshot / journal imagesInternal documents directorySnapshots screen
Notes, saved linksSharedPreferencesCore functionality
Vault entries (passwords, usernames)SharedPreferencesPassword manager
ExpensesSharedPreferencesExpense tracking
Profile (name, DOB, phone, currency)SharedPreferencesPersonalisation
Custom alarm soundInternal documents directoryAlarm notifications
App preferences (theme, accent, last screen)SharedPreferencesUI state

3. What we do NOT collect

  • No analytics or usage statistics
  • No advertising SDKs or tracking libraries
  • No data transmitted to any server or third party
  • No account or registration required
  • No access to contacts, call logs, SMS, or location

No permission is used for data collection or advertising.

5. Data storage and security

All data is stored in Android's sandboxed SharedPreferences and internal documents directory — inaccessible to other apps under normal conditions and never transmitted off the device.

Data is not encrypted at rest. On a rooted device, stored data may be readable. We recommend setting a device screen lock.

6. Backup files

The "Export data" feature creates a JSON file on your device and opens the native share sheet. The app does not upload this file anywhere. You choose where to save it. You are responsible for the security of exported backup files.

7. Third-party services

Only Me does not integrate any analytics, advertising, crash-reporting, or cloud-sync services. Open-source Flutter packages (flutter_local_notifications, shared_preferences, image_picker, etc.) operate locally only and do not send data to external servers.

8. Children's privacy

Only Me does not knowingly collect information from children. The app has no registration and collects no data that leaves the device, making it suitable for users of all ages.

9. Data deletion

Delete all app data at any time by:

  • Deleting individual items within the app
  • Android Settings → Apps → Only Me → Storage → Clear Data
  • Uninstalling the app (all data is permanently deleted)

10. Changes to this policy

Material changes will be included in the next app update. The "Last updated" date will be revised. Continued use after an update constitutes acceptance.
